# Business brief

## What the business is

A two-person exterior home services business on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, based in Aroona. One person works the ladder (gutters, windows, gutter guard); the other runs the washer (house soft wash, driveways and paths). Most jobs are finished in one morning.

Services:

- House soft washing: walls, eaves, frames. Low pressure with a cleaning solution, safe on paint, render, brick, weatherboard and Colorbond.
- Driveway and path pressure cleaning, using a rotary surface cleaner for an even finish.
- Gutter cleaning: debris bagged and removed, downpipes flushed.
- Exterior window cleaning: glass, frames, screens rinsed.
- Fence and retaining wall cleaning: Colorbond, timber and besser block, priced per metre per side, from $120. Added 2 Nov 2026; also offered as an add-on to any bundle.
- Gutter guard supply and installation: aluminium mesh, fixed price by gutter length, gutter clean included.

Not offered yet: roof washing, solar panel cleaning, interior windows. (Can be listed as "coming soon" or left off.)

## How customers find us

Almost all customers come from our own flyers, delivered to every letterbox in a suburb by our flyer delivery business. Each flyer has a QR code that opens the website with a `?src=` tag for that street group. The site's main job is to turn a phone visit from a flyer into a booked day with a deposit.

## Who it's for

Owner-occupiers of detached houses in established, leafy suburbs: Buderim, Little Mountain, Aroona, Mountain Creek, Currimundi, Pelican Waters. Many are older owners who don't want to be on a ladder, or busy families who want the whole outside done in one go. House values are mostly $1.2M to $1.6M.

## Positioning

- **The whole exterior in one morning, one fixed price.** No quote visit, no call-out fee.
- **Why it's cheaper:** we already deliver to your street, and we book whole streets on the same days, so there's no travel cost to pass on.
- **Local and accountable:** two locals, the same two people on every job.
- **Careful with your home:** soft wash on painted surfaces, not high pressure; wash water kept out of the street drains.

## Tone

Plain, friendly, confident. Short sentences. Talk about the customer's house, not about us. No hype words ("premium", "elite", "unbeatable"). Prices shown up front.
